# Hydrangea interpretation — not shipped

Read the complete 14-page [John Smith guide to Shuzo Fujimoto's Hydrangea](https://cfcorigami.com/sites/default/files/diagrams/igaribi/hydrangea_john_smith.pdf) visually on 2026-10-25. The downloaded reference and rendered pages are ignored local research files, not APK assets.

## Sequence understood from the guide

- Steps 1–3: prepare the grid and diagonal creases.
- Steps 4–6: collapse the crossed box pleat, then flatten.
- Steps 7–12: establish and spread a smaller central layer; repeat around the sheet.
- Steps 13–21: sink corners and prepare the petal flaps.
- Steps 22–26: invert the pyramidal pockets, flatten their halves, repeat around the layer.
- Steps 27–28: shape the petals; the construction can repeat for additional layers.
- Steps 29–33: interlock layers and finish the outer petals.

## What is still missing
